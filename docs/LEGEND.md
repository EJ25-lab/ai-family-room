# THE LEGEND
**AI Family Room · EdJustice Collective · brand wayfinding canon**

Every emoji and section icon below is **locked**. Use these — and only these — in any future asset (site copy, welcome emails, Field Notes, share kit, decks, social posts).

If you need a new symbol, add it to this doc first.

---

## AUDIENCE TAGS

These six emojis are the *only* ones used to flag who content is for. They appear in nav, dropdowns, mission cards, share kit, welcome emails, and Field Notes.

| Emoji | Audience | Track color | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🤎 | **Parents** navigating the system | Rose `#D4736A` | School emails, IEP/504 work, hard conversations, accommodations |
| 🎖 | **Military spouses** | Purple `#7B5BA6` | PCS, career gaps, deployment, base life, OPSEC |
| ⭐ | **Transitioning service members** | Navy `#1E3A5F` | Post-team workflows, independent operations, civilian rebuild |
| 🏫 | **Educators & leaders** | Teal `#1ABCB0` | Classroom use, grace-first AI, district + school leader contexts |
| 🌱 | **Brand new / Foundations** | Teal `#1ABCB0` | First-time AI users, the door, prompt basics |
| ✨ | **Build Your Superpower** | Orange `#E76F51` | Skill-up missions, going from beginner to confident |

**Hex codes locked.** Track color and audience emoji always pair. Never mix.

**Why 🤎 for parents:** the brown heart represents the families this room was built for and reflects the community at the heart of this work. It carries identity, not just sentiment.

## FIELD NOTES SECTION ICONS

Distinct from audience tags. These mark **what kind of content** the reader is in — used only inside Field Notes issues.

| Icon | Section | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 💡 | **ONE THING TO TRY** | This month's prompt — the headline tip |
| 📍 | **WHAT'S NEW IN THE ROOM** | Site updates, new missions, new tracks |
| 🔭 | **ONE PATTERN** | What we observe in the room (no individual stories — patterns only) |
| ✍️ | **FROM THE CHIEF** | Personal note in the Chief's voice (3–4 sentences) |
| ✅ | **THREE EASY ACTIONS** | What to do this week |

Field notes language anchored in observation: clipboards, telescopes, pencils.

---

## FIELD NOTES MASTHEAD

```
📋 FIELD NOTES
from The Collective
Vol. N · Month Year
```

Clipboard left of wordmark. *from The Collective* attribution always rendered as italic, lighter weight. Volume + date always third line.

---

## HOW TO COMBINE TAGS AND ICONS

When the headline tip is parent-focused: `🤎 💡 ONE THING TO TRY`
When the headline tip is mil-spouse-focused: `🎖 💡 ONE THING TO TRY`
A "What's New" line for parents only: `🤎 Parent Intel just launched. [...]`
A multi-audience update: `🎖 ⭐ 🏫 More tracks coming.`

The audience emoji always comes **before** the section icon.
