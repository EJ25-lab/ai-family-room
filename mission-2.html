✨ Build Your Superpower Track Mission 02

Write a love note from memory.

Pick a person. Pick a moment. Give Claude two sentences. You'll have something they keep forever in three minutes.

⚡ +50 XP ❤️ No badge — just heart 🔧 Claude

Three steps. You're done in three minutes.

1
Pick one moment. Tell Claude in two sentences.

Not a love story. Not a poem. One memory. The way she stirred the pot. The way he laughed at his own jokes. How she sang you the same song every birthday. Two sentences. Move on.

✍️ Try this — copy and paste it into Claude

"I want to write a love note to my grandmother for her birthday. The memory I keep coming back to is how she made cornbread every Sunday and let me pour the buttermilk. That smell is still home to me. Help me turn that into something I can give her."

Open Claude →
2
Tell Claude who it's to and how it should feel.

Warm? Formal? Funny? Tender? Short enough for a card or long enough for a letter? Claude doesn't know your family. You do. Tell it.

✨ Follow up in the same Claude chat

"Make it warm but not flowery. Short enough to fit on a card. Sound like a granddaughter who is grown now but still misses being small in her kitchen."

3
Ask for three versions. Keep the one that hits.

The first one Claude writes will be good. The second will be different. The third will surprise you. Pick the one that makes you pause. Edit it. Send it. Frame it. Put it in a card.

Final prompt to Claude

"Give me three short versions — each with a different tone. One warm, one playful, one quiet."

What just happened

You didn't ask Claude to write a love note. You gave it one specific memory and asked it to translate. That's the real skill — knowing the value lives in the detail you already carry, not in the AI. You already had the love. Claude just helped you find the words.

You wrote it. Now send it.

No badge for this one on purpose. Some things you do for the person, not the points. You still get +50 XP.
