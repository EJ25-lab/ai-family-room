✨ Build Your Superpower Track Mission 05

Brain dump to doc in 60 seconds.

Talk it. Type it. Yell at your phone. Claude turns the mess into a clean one-page doc you can actually send.

⚡ +50 XP 📄 Page Turner Badge 🔧 Claude

Three steps. You are done in five minutes.

1
Get every angry, scrambled thought OUT of your head.

Use your phone's voice typing. Or just type it messy. Punctuation does not matter. Spelling does not matter. Get it all out. The IEP request. The HOA complaint. The doctor follow-up. The thing you have been chewing on for a week.

🎤 START WITH ONE OF THESE — DUMP IT IN THE BOX BELOW

"my son's accommodations are slipping through the cracks the extended time isn't landing on every quiz his teacher has 90 kids and i know its hard i've asked twice and i need a clear system that protects him without overloading her i want to partner with the iep team to fix this"

2
Send the mess to Claude with one line of instruction.

Copy your dump. Open Claude. Paste it. Then tell Claude exactly what to do with it. The dump is the data. The instruction is the magic.

✍️ PASTE THIS UNDER YOUR DUMP

"Turn this into a professional one-page letter to my son's IEP team. Keep my voice but make it clear, calm, and partnership-focused. Acknowledge the team is working hard, name what's slipping, propose a specific next step we can build together, and offer a meeting time."

Open Claude →
3
Read what Claude built. Fix one line. Send it.

Claude wrote a real letter. Read it. Change one or two things to make it sound like you. That is it. Send it. Print it. Email it. Take it to the meeting.

PASTE CLAUDE'S DOC HERE FOR YOUR RECORDS
WHAT JUST HAPPENED

You took 60 seconds of frustrated thought and turned it into a real document. That gap — between what you feel and what reads as partnership on paper — is what stops so many parents and spouses from being heard the first time. You just closed it. Now you can do it every time.

From your head to a real document. In minutes.

This is the move that turns AI from a toy into a partner. The doc you just made? Save it. You will use this pattern again.
