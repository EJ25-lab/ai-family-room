✨ Build Your Superpower Track Mission 06

Family stories into a deck.

The recipes. The deployments. The birth stories. The lessons the elders keep saying. Turn them into slides your kids will actually keep.

⚡ +50 XP 🎞 Story Keeper Badge 🔧 Claude + Gamma

Three steps. You leave with a real deck.

1
List five stories you don't want to lose.

Not memoir. Not history. Five lines. The thing Grandma always said. The deployment she remembers most. The first day of school nobody talked about. The recipe that has no recipe.

📝 Your five stories — just titles, no detail yet
2
Tell Claude to turn each into three slides of content.

Open Claude. Paste your five titles. Then give it the instruction below. It will draft three slides per story — a title slide, a story slide, and a lesson slide. Fifteen slides in two minutes.

✍️ Paste this under your five titles

"For each of these five family stories, write 3 slides: (1) a title slide with a strong headline, (2) a story slide with 3-4 bullet points of what happened in plain spoken language, and (3) a lesson slide — one sentence on what this story teaches the next generation. Format it cleanly so I can paste it straight into Gamma."

Open Claude →
3
Paste it into Gamma. Hit Generate. Done.

Gamma turns Claude's text into a real deck with images and design in under a minute. Pick a theme that feels like your family. Edit any slide you want to. Then save it. Email it. Print it. Put it in the family group chat.

Open Gamma and paste Claude's output

In Gamma, choose "Paste in text" → paste everything Claude gave you → pick a theme → Generate.

Open Gamma →
What just happened

You just preserved five family stories in a format the next generation will actually open. The hardest part was never the design. It was knowing what to keep. You knew. You wrote five lines. The rest was the tools doing their job. Most people never make this deck. You did.

You made a thing your family will keep.

That's the real win. The XP and the badge are just for showing up. Save the deck somewhere everyone can find it.
