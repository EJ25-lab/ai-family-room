Weak vs. Strong.

Type something vague. Watch NeuralWriter fix it. That gap is the skill.

⚡ +50 XP 🌿 First Spark Badge 🔧 NeuralWriter

Three steps. You are done in five minutes.

1
Type something rough into NeuralWriter

Go to NeuralWriter's Prompt Generator. Type anything vague — something you actually need help with this week. Do not try to make it good. That is the point.

"Help me write an email to my child's new teacher."

Open NeuralWriter →

Hit Generate. Read what came back.

NeuralWriter added specifics, context, and tone to your vague thought. That is the whole skill — made visible in one click. Paste what it gave you in the box below.

Take that polished prompt to Claude.
