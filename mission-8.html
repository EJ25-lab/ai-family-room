✨ Build Your Superpower Track Mission 08

The follow-up superpower.

The conversation you want to keep alive — when inboxes are buried, schedules slip, kids are sick. One email. Stays warm. Keeps it moving.

⚡ +50 XP 🔁 Follow-Up Queen Badge 🔧 Claude

Three steps. Conversation stays alive.

1
Pick the conversation you want to bring back to life.

The teacher juggling 90 kids whose inbox got away from her. The doctor's office where messages stack up. The landlord. The insurance person. The VA caseworker. Pick the one that matters most this week. Assume everyone is overloaded. Tell Claude the situation in 3 lines.

✍️ PASTE THIS INTO CLAUDE — FILL IN YOUR DETAILS

"I emailed a teacher 11 days ago about a learning support plan for one of my kids. I know she is juggling a full class load — I want to follow up warmly and helpfully. Help me write a short, kind message that names the timeline, offers to schedule whenever works for her, and reminds her I am her partner in this, not adding to her stack."

Open Claude →
2
Pick the tone. Tell Claude exactly which one.

The same situation needs a different email depending on how far things have gone. Pick one. Then tell Claude.

Warm First nudge — still friendly
Firm Second nudge — calm but serious
Formal Third nudge — on paper, on record
✨ FOLLOW UP IN CLAUDE WITH

"Write three versions of this follow-up — one warm, one firm, one formal — so I can pick the one that fits where we are."

3
Pick your version. Send it. Save the others.

Claude wrote three versions. Pick the one that matches your situation. Send it from your email. Save the other two. If they ghost you again, the next one is already written.

WHAT JUST HAPPENED

You just kept the conversation going when it would have been easier to let it drop. The follow-up was not the problem — writing it warmly when you are frustrated was. Now you have a template you can run again next week. And the week after. That is a system, not a hope. Everyone on the other end is also doing their best.

You stayed in the room.

That is the win. The follow-up is the move that keeps the partnership alive when life gets loud. Save the prompt. Reuse it. Teach it to a friend.
