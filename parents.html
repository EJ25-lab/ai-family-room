🦎 Parent Intel · For parents navigating the system

You are not behind.
The system is opaque. Here are the words and the doors.

Built for the parents holding it together — the school emails, the accommodation requests, the hard conversations, the meetings that decide your kid's year. Two missions. Real artifacts. The doors are open.

📝P-1 · Accommodation Translator 💬P-2 · Hard Conversation Prep 🦎Resources & support
Mission P-1 · 8 minutes

The Accommodation Translator.

Take the IEP or 504 jargon you don’t fully understand. Turn it into plain language. Walk out with a partnership email to the case manager that asks for what your kid actually needs.

Mission P-1
The Accommodation Translator

You shouldn’t need a law degree to understand your kid’s plan. AI translates the dense paragraphs into "what this means for my Tuesday," then helps you write a warm, specific email that gets a response.

Mission P-2 · 10 minutes

The Hard Conversation Prep.

Walk into the pediatrician, counselor, or principal’s office knowing what you came to say. AI helps you organize what you’ve been noticing, what you’re worried about, and what you’re asking for — on one page you can bring with you.

Mission P-2
The Hard Conversation Prep

For the parent who freezes in those rooms because the stakes are too high to wing it. AI listens, organizes, and hands you a clean one-pager: here’s what I’ve seen, here’s what concerns me, here’s what I’m asking for.
