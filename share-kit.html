What this is

A free, self-guided AI learning room.

Built for parents, military spouses, transitioning service members, and educators. No login. No paywall. No data sold. Built to stay open.

Visitors pick a mission. They do the thing. They walk away with a real artifact — an email they can send, a resume bullet they can use, a one-pager for the meeting they were dreading.

Who it's for

If you know any of these people, send them in.

🦋

Parents

Navigating school systems, accommodation requests, and hard conversations about their kids.

🎖️

Military spouses

Managing PCS moves, career gaps, deployment seasons, and base life.

⭐

Transitioning service members

Senior leaders rebuilding workflows after a career of command. No more staff. AI is the new staff.

🏫

Educators & leaders

Looking for grace, not gimmicks. Real classroom and leadership use.

🧐

Anyone catching up on AI

People who've been told AI is important but never given a door in.

What people walk away with

Real artifacts. Not theory.

  • The school email that finally gets answered
  • The resume bullets that translate a six-year PCS gap into leadership
  • The plain-language version of the IEP / 504 plan
  • The one-pager for the meeting they'd been dreading
  • The conversation map for talking to a teacher, counselor, or pediatrician
  • The transition brief for their first 90 days out of uniform

Why it's free

Built on the belief that technology should amplify every voice — not just the ones with resources.

This is a community offering, not a product. There's nothing to upsell. There's no catch. Built independently by EdJustice Collective to support the communities that get talked at about AI — and rarely taught with it.

How to share

Three ways. Pick whichever fits.

Forward this kit

Print, save as PDF, or send the URL of this page to colleagues, partners, or anyone you support.

Send the link

aifamilyroom.com — that's the whole address. No login. No fine print. Memorable enough to drop in a text.

Host a session

Run a 30-minute "we're doing this together" session for your group. Email us — we'll send a facilitator outline.
