Tool 01 of 03 · the main event
Claude
"The AI that thinks out loud with you"

Claude is the centerpiece of everything we do in the AI Family Room. What makes it different is that it shows its reasoning — it explains what it's doing and why, so you stay in the driver's seat. It's conversational, patient, and genuinely good at the real-life communication families need: school emails, advocacy letters, career narratives, community messages. It doesn't just give you an answer; it thinks through the problem with you.

Tool 02 of 03
NeuralWriter
"Your writing warm-up"

NeuralWriter is where you start when you're not sure what to say. Its Prompt Generator takes a rough thought — even just a few words — and shapes it into a clear, specific request before you take it anywhere else. Think of it as the warm-up stretch before the workout. No account needed for basic tools, which makes it the lowest-friction first step in the room.

▶ See it in action
Prompting NeuralWriter — how a vague thought becomes a structured request
🔒No account needed for basic tools. 10,000-character limit on the free tier. You're not creating a login or sharing personal information to get started.
Free to startAdvanced from $19/mo
Try NeuralWriter →
💡
How we use it

Use NeuralWriter's Prompt Generator to turn a vague idea into a structured request. Then copy that polished prompt and bring it to Claude. NeuralWriter does the shaping. Claude does the thinking.

What you can do with it
🖊️
Turn a vague idea into a real ask

Type "help with email" and watch it become a specific, contextualized request Claude can actually work with.

🌟
Practice the 3 Keys before you need them

Specific, context, tone — NeuralWriter applies all three automatically so you can see what a strong prompt looks like.

⚡
Get unstuck in under a minute

When you sit down and your mind goes blank, NeuralWriter gives you a starting point without requiring you to already know what to ask.

🎓
Build the skill, not just the output

Every time you use it you're learning what good prompts look like. After a few sessions you won't need it anymore — and that's the point.

Tool 03 of 03
Gamma
"Instant docs, decks, and pages"

Gamma takes one solid sentence and turns it into a beautiful, structured visual document in seconds. It handles the design so you don't have to — no more staring at a blank slide wondering what font to use. You bring the idea. Gamma brings the presentation. Whether it's a family reunion plan, a side-hustle pitch, or an FRG briefing, it looks professional immediately.
