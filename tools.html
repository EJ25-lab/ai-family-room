THE TOOLS

Three tools.
Zero overwhelm.

Free to start. No downloads. Sign in with Google and you are halfway there. Each tool does one job really well — and together they cover almost everything your family needs.

TOOL 01 OF 03
NeuralWriter
"Your writing warm-up"

NeuralWriter is where you start when you are not sure what to say. Its Prompt Generator takes a rough thought — even just a few words — and shapes it into a clear, specific request before you take it anywhere else. Think of it as the warm-up stretch before the workout. No account needed for basic tools, which makes it the lowest-friction first step in the room.

🔒 No account needed for basic tools. 10,000 character limit on the free tier. You are not creating a login or sharing personal information to get started.
Free to start Advanced features from $19/mo
Try NeuralWriter →
💡
How we use it

Use NeuralWriter's Prompt Generator to turn a vague idea into a structured request. Then copy that polished prompt and bring it to Claude. NeuralWriter does the shaping. Claude does the thinking.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH IT
🖊
Turn a vague idea into a real ask

Type "help with email" and watch it become a specific, contextualized request Claude can actually work with.

🌟
Practice the 3 Keys before you need them

Specific, context, tone — NeuralWriter applies all three automatically so you can see what a strong prompt looks like.

⚡
Get unstuck in under a minute

When you sit down and your mind goes blank, NeuralWriter gives you a starting point without requiring you to already know what to ask.

🎓
Build the skill, not just the output

Every time you use it you are learning what good prompts look like. After a few sessions you will not need it anymore — and that is the point.

✨ TRY IT NOW

"Help me write an email to my child's new school after we moved."

TOOL 02 OF 03 · OUR MAIN TOOL
Claude
"The AI that thinks out loud with you"

Claude is the centerpiece of everything we do in the AI Family Room. What makes it different from other AI tools is that it shows its reasoning — it explains what it is doing and why, which means you stay in the driver's seat. It is conversational, patient, and genuinely good at the kind of real-life communication that families need: school emails, advocacy letters, career narratives, community messages. It does not just give you an answer; it thinks through the problem with you.

🔒 Free conversations on claude.ai are not used to train the model by default. You are not handing over your data just by using it.
Free Pro from $20/mo
Open Claude → Try the Art of the Ask first
🚀
The easy button

Use "Continue with Google" to sign in. If you have a Gmail account you are already halfway there — no new password, no new account to manage.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH IT
💌
Write emails that actually get responses

From the new teacher introduction to the landlord SCRA letter — Claude drafts it and you edit until it sounds like you.

💼
Explain a career gap without apologizing

Every PCS was a leadership role. Claude helps you turn four moves and three career pauses into the professional story they actually are.

🏠
Translate TRICARE, SCRA, and military bureaucracy

Paste any document and ask Claude to explain it in plain English. Then ask it to help you draft a response or an appeal.

🤝
Practice hard conversations before you have them

Ask Claude to roleplay the other side — the principal, the landlord, the HR manager. Walk in prepared instead of nervous.

💡
Get a second opinion without judgment

Claude does not get tired, does not make you feel silly for asking, and does not charge by the hour. Ask it anything.

✨ TRY IT NOW

TOOL 03 OF 03
Gamma
"Instant docs, decks, and pages"

Gamma takes one solid sentence and turns it into a beautiful, structured visual document in seconds. It handles the design so you do not have to — which means no more staring at a blank PowerPoint slide wondering what font to use. You bring the idea. Gamma brings the presentation. Whether it is a family reunion plan, a side hustle pitch, or an FRG briefing, it looks professional immediately.

🔒 400 free credits to explore and create. Sign in with Google is recommended for the smoothest experience. No "Made with Gamma" badge on the Plus plan.
Free (400 credits) Plus $8–$10/mo
Try Gamma →
🌟
The workflow

Build your idea in Claude first. Get the content right. Then bring that polished content to Gamma to make it beautiful. Claude does the thinking. Gamma does the design.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH IT
🌟
Build a slide deck from one sentence

📄

🏠
Plan a family event with a real document

Family reunion, vacation itinerary, household budget overview — anything that benefits from structure and looks good shared.

🚀
Pitch a side hustle or community idea

You have the idea. Gamma gives it a professional package. Show up to any meeting looking prepared without spending hours preparing.
